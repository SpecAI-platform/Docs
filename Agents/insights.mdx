The **Insights Agent** (`services/agents/insights`) doesn't answer a specific question — it proactively analyzes the entire project knowledge base and surfaces things you didn't know to ask about.

## What it produces

### Risk analysis

Identifies potential problems before they happen by looking for:

- Requirements that are vague, contradictory, or under-specified
- Commitments made in emails that aren't reflected in formal specs
- Dependencies on external parties or systems with no contingency
- Scope that has grown beyond what was originally agreed

### Gap analysis

Finds what's **missing** from the knowledge base:

- Areas where decisions were referenced but never documented
- Requirements categories present in some documents but absent in others
- Open questions from meetings that were never answered or captured

### Project state breakdowns

Pivot-table-style summaries that give a structured view of project health:

- Requirements by status (defined / unclear / missing)
- Action items by owner and deadline
- Content coverage by topic area (how much ingested context exists per domain)

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger">
    User clicks "Run Insights" or the scheduler triggers a periodic analysis. Core dispatches a job to QStash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Broad retrieval">
    Unlike targeted agents, Insights runs **multiple broad vector queries** across different topic areas to get a comprehensive view of the knowledge base — not just the most similar chunk to one question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze and categorize">
    The LLM is prompted to act as a project analyst: identify risks, flag gaps, and structure what it finds into the output categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store results">
    Insights output is written to Postgres (structured JSON), surfaced in the dashboard as an expandable panel, and updated each time the agent runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
